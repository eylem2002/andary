# QA checks — 2026-11-17

Independent QA pass over Andary (branch `feat/hud-and-app-shell` and the stack below it), done as
an outside tester: every role, every feature, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), performance, licences
and config lint. Issues found were fixed with tests where that made sense; what is left is listed
at the end. A security review ran in parallel in `server/`, `hooks/` and `bin/`; this pass kept out
of those folders unless a bug forced it.

Summary: 8 issues found and fixed (2 medium user-facing bugs, 1 medium CLS problem, 1 medium
release-script hazard, 4 low); 6 of the fixes come with new automated tests that fail without
them, the release-script and docs fixes were checked by hand. One environment-only rendering
problem was investigated and left for a real-GPU check. Lighthouse accessibility is 100 on desktop
and mobile (mobile was 98), mobile CLS went from 0.127 to 0.034–0.056, and heap and DOM level off
over 5 minutes at 4×.

## 5. Lighthouse (`npm run perf`: Lighthouse 13.6.0, `/?demo=1`, one run per preset)

SwiftShader draws the 3D town on the CPU in this container, and Lighthouse's mobile preset adds 4×
CPU throttling on top, so Performance is dominated by the WebGL frame loop (Total Blocking Time
30 s desktop / 160 s mobile, Time to Interactive 40 s / 195 s). Run-to-run noise is about ±8
points. Judge the performance column by its sub-metrics (CLS in particular), not by its value.

| Round | When | Desktop P · A · BP · SEO | Mobile P · A · BP · SEO | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Baseline | 46 · 100 · 100 · 100 | 31 · 98 · 100 · 100 | Mobile: `heading-order` fails; CLS 0.127 (one shift: the toolbar, 0.127) |
| 2 | After P1 + A3 | 38 · 100 · 100 · 100 | 35 · **100** · 100 · 100 | Mobile CLS 0.127 → 0.034; desktop CLS 0.01 → 0.011 (noise) |
| 3 | Final tree (all fixes) | 38 · 100 · 100 · 100 | 35 · 100 · 100 · 100 | CLS 0.018 desktop, 0.056 mobile (both under 0.1; the rest is crew rows being inserted while the demo fills up) |

Metrics (round 1 → round 2): desktop FCP 0.3 s → 0.3 s, LCP 2.6 s → 4.3 s (noise; the LCP element
is a crew line that appears when the demo's first crew arrives), CLS 0.01 → 0.011; mobile FCP
1.1 s → 1.1 s, LCP 8.3 s → 9.1 s, CLS 0.127 → 0.034.

Not changed, by design: "minify JS/CSS", "unused JavaScript" and "cache lifetimes" (no build step,
ADR 0002; `vendor/` is cached for a day, everything else revalidates with an ETag). Not changed,
too large for a QA pass: the demo starts only once the 3D world is ready (so the director misses no
event), which delays the first HUD content on slow devices; starting the store first and letting
the director catch up from the store's state would bring LCP forward.
