# QA checks — 2026-11-20 (game view)

Checks for `feat/game-view-director-camera`: the director camera, the full-screen town and the
bigger figures, after the fixes from the independent review
([`../reviews/game-view-review-2026-11-20.md`](../reviews/game-view-review-2026-11-20.md)). Same
environment as the 2026-11-17 checks: Linux container, 2 vCPU, no GPU, Node 22.23.4, Chrome 153
with SwiftShader (the 3D town runs at 2–3 fps here, so performance numbers are depressed).

## Visual checks

Screenshots at 1440×900 and 1280×720 (director close-ups, wide shots, the caption, day and night),
660 px (toolbar wraps to two rows, boards sit above it, no caption), 800 px full screen (one-row
toolbar, caption above it) and a 375 px phone (two-row toolbar, caption above it). A 46-second
recording was checked frame by frame: the director follows crews off the bus, helpers out of the
portal, teammates at work, and pulls back for wide shots.

The first recordings of this change looked wrong (subjects hidden in the bus, agents frozen):
the recorder slowed the page clock twice, once by script and once through DevTools' animation
playback rate, so the 3D world ran at a fraction of the page's speed. The app was not at fault;
the recorder now slows animation frames only once, and the world's clock was checked to match the
page's (46.0 s of town time in 46 s of page time).
